# Assumptions

Public retailer-portal material was used only as a workflow reference. The portal remains a generic enterprise B2B retailer and wholesale distribution sample, with no copied branding, layouts, private screens, legal text, or product-specific content.

- Business domain: multi-location retailer purchasing contract supplies from a distributor.
- Roles: account admin, buyer, approver, finance, and viewer.
- Permissions: products, orders, invoices, customers, account, and user management.
- Order statuses: submitted, approved, fulfilling, shipped, delivered, and cancelled.
- Invoice statuses: open, partially paid, paid, overdue, and void.
- Pricing: contract price overrides list price when present.
- Discounts: 5 percent discount above 5,000 USD subtotal.
- Shipping: free above 1,500 USD subtotal, otherwise 85 USD.
- Taxes: 8.25 percent unless the account is tax exempt.
- Cancellation is allowed only before fulfillment/shipping.
- Data is synthetic and must not be interpreted as real customer data.
